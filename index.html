7.1 Unit Sales Tax — A. Meaning 工作紙第 1 頁頂部
A unit sales tax is imposed when the same amount of tax is levied on each unit of output.
💡 廣東話理解：無論貨品賣幾多錢，政府對每件賣出嘅貨品徵收相同金額嘅稅。
Target: Producers (sellers)
Prediction: $2 per-unit sales tax = Price increases by $2?
🤔 先記低大家嘅猜測！ 政府向賣家每件收 $2 稅，係咪代表市價一定會加足 $2？即刻用 Part B 驗證！

Tax Burden & Gov Tax Revenue
📌 Government tax revenue = $2 × 20 units = $40
• Consumers’ tax burden = ($4 − $3) × 20 units = $20
• Producers’ tax burden = ($3 − $2) × 20 units = $20
• Government tax revenue = $20 + $20 = $40
C. General Conclusions
1. $x per-unit sales tax is not necessarily result in $x increase in market price 例：$2 稅 → P 只升 $1
2. Q decreases  &  P increases Q: 30→20 | P: $3→$4
3. Consumers’ total expenditure may increase, decrease or remain unchanged (depends on Ed) 視乎需求彈性 Ed
4. The price and total revenue that producers actually received decrease 必定減少 ($90→$40)
Challenging Questions: • When are conclusions invalid? • What determines higher tax burden?

If the government imposed a $2 per unit tax on Good X,

📊 新舊均衡：稅前 P = $10, Q = 100；加 $2 稅後 P = $11, Q = 90（賣家實收 = $9）。
A. sellers’ revenue net of tax would become $990.
✘ 錯誤｜實收 = ($11 − $2) × 90 = $810。（⚠️ $990 係未扣稅總收入）
B. tax burden borne by the sellers would be $90.
✔ 正確｜($10 − $9) × 90 units = $1 × 90 = $90。
C. the total tax revenue would be $200.
✘ 錯誤｜$2 × 90 units = $180。（⚠️ 誤用稅前舊成交量 100 件）
D. buyers’ total expenditure on Good X would decrease by $40.
✘ 錯誤｜TE 由 $1,000 跌至 $990 → 減少 $10，並非 $40。
